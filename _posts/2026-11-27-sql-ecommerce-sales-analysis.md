## Overview

A PostgreSQL analysis of e-commerce sales, profitability, customer value, and sales trends. The project uses orders, order details, and monthly sales targets to identify what drives performance and where the business loses money.

## What I Analyzed

- Customer analysis and segmentation, with most customers falling into a low-value segment.
- Category and sub-category profitability — Clothing had the highest margin, while Furniture lagged; Tables and Electronic Games were loss-making.
- State- and city-level performance, including regions reporting losses.
- Monthly sales trends, with peaks around November to January.
- Actual sales compared with targets, where Electronics performed strongly.
- Order-level profitability, including orders mixing profitable and loss-making items.

## Tools and Techniques

- PostgreSQL and pgAdmin
- SQL joins, aggregations, grouping, and CASE statements
- Business insight generation from query results
