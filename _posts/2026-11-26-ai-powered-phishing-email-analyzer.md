## Overview

An AI-powered application designed to analyze suspicious phishing emails, identify potential threats, and assist in understanding malicious email content.

## Objectives

- Analyze suspicious email messages.
- Identify potential phishing indicators.
- Understand suspicious links and email content.
- Use AI to support phishing email investigations.

## Technologies

- Python
- Streamlit
- Ollama
