## Overview

An AI-powered Security Operations Center (SOC) investigation platform designed to assist security analysts in investigating and understanding security alerts.

## Objectives

- Explore how AI can support SOC investigations.
- Organize security alerts for investigation.
- Generate incident reports to support security analysis.

## Technologies

- Python
- Streamlit
- Flask
- Ollama
