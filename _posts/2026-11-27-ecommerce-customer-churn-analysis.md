## Overview

An Excel-based analysis of customer churn on an e-commerce platform, using roughly 50,000 customer records to understand which behaviors are associated with customers leaving.

## What I Analyzed

- Engagement patterns — retained customers had higher login frequency, longer sessions, and better engagement scores.
- Cart abandonment — customers with an 80–100% abandonment rate had an estimated churn rate of about 75%.
- Customer service interactions — more interactions were associated with higher churn.
- Customer value — high-value customers had an estimated churn rate of 47%.
- Overall churn — 14,449 of 49,999 customers churned, a rate of 28.90%.

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel — data cleaning, preparation, PivotTables, charts, and an interactive dashboard
- Customer categorization into behavioral and value ranges
- Exploratory analysis comparing churned and retained customers
