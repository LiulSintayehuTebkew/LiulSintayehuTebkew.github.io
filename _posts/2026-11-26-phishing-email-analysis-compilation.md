## Overview

A collection of practical phishing email analysis exercises focused on examining suspicious email messages, identifying phishing indicators, and understanding common email-based cyber threats.

## Objectives

- Analyze suspicious email messages.
- Identify common phishing techniques.
- Examine email headers and sender information.
- Identify suspicious links and attachments.
- Understand techniques used in email-based attacks.

## Technologies

- Email Header Analysis
- URL Analysis
- Cyber Threat Intelligence
