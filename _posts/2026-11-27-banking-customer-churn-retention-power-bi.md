## Overview

An interactive Power BI analysis of banking customer churn, built from related Excel tables organized into a star schema. The project explores which customer segments are most likely to leave and where retention efforts should be focused.

## What I Analyzed

- Overall churn and retention rates — about 20% of customers exited, with 7,963 of 10,000 retained.
- Churn by gender, credit card ownership, geography, age group, and credit score.
- France had the highest number of exited customers, and churn was concentrated among customers aged 30–45.
- Non-credit-card holders and customers with Fair or Poor credit scores showed higher churn.
- Built a Power BI dashboard to make the findings easy to explore.

## Tools and Techniques

- Power BI — data modeling, visualization, and dashboards
- Microsoft Excel — dataset preparation and storage
- Star schema data model over customer, demographic, geographic, and churn tables
